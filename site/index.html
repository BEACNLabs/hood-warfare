Funding

Where the money comes from

Every USDC paid to a player comes out of the rewards pool, and the pool is filled from exactly two places. Nothing is minted to pay you, and nothing is promised that the pool does not already hold.

Source 1

The treasury

Seeded by the team before Season 1 and topped up at the start of every season. It is the floor under the ladder: the amount the tiers are sized against on day one.

Source 2

Creator fees

A fixed share of the creator fees the token earns on every trade flows into the pool each week, on-chain. The more the block trades, the more the next season pays.

The rule

Payouts never exceed the pool. Tiers are sized from the pool's balance when a season opens. Bounties are paid from the same pool as they happen. If the pool runs ahead, the next season's tiers go up; the current season's never move.

USDC on Solana, to the wallet you played with. Connect a wallet to play ranked. Ladder tiers settle every Sunday; bounties settle with the ladder. Claims are yours to take any time after settlement.

The game

Twenty houses, two crews

A residential block on Chicago's south side at blue hour. Opps in red, homies in blue, six a side. An AK-47 and an RPG-7. The houses, the garden walls and the roofs are real physics: a rocket opens a front wall and the bricks land in the yard.

Wasted
Destruction

Every wall is thousands of welded bricks

Nothing is scripted. Thirteen thousand bricks and panels are simulated on your GPU; a rocket tears the welds, the wall comes apart where you hit it, the roof drops where nothing holds it, and the rubble stays where it falls for the rest of the match.

Crews and abilities

Opps, homies, three calls to make

Kills charge three abilities. Snitch marks every enemy on the block for twelve seconds. Homies drops two of your crew at your back. Bomber sends a runner at the nearest opp. First team to a hundred points, or the most when the ten minutes are up.

Browser only

No download. Phone or desktop

Open the link and play. Touch controls on a phone, mouse and keyboard on a desktop, the same block and the same ladder for both. Sound by a real foley set, from the AK's slap-back off the houses to the bricks coming down.
