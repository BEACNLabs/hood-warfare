Hood Warfare 2 is blowing up on X and costs $5.39 on Steam.

fuck it. we built our own browser version, made it free and added a prize pool so you can actually get paid to play 😭

no download. no $5. works on desktop and phone.

Same concept: Chicago block at night. Opps in red, homies in blue. 6v6. You get an AK and an RPG.

Kills charge 3 abilities:
- Snitch reveals every opp on the map
- Homies spawns two of your crew
- Bomber sends a runner at the nearest enemy

The part I spent way too much time on is the destruction.

12,852 individual bricks and panels running through GPU physics. RPG a house and it blows open exactly where you hit it. The rubble actually stays.

Built with Three.js, real night-sky HDRI lighting and characters modelled and rigged in Blender.

Also added a weekly ranked ladder because apparently blowing up your friends' houses wasn't enough.

10 points per kill, 50 per win. Rankings reset every Sunday. Top 1,000 players qualify for USDC prizes, plus MVP and Rampage bonuses.

We put $500 of our own money into the first prize pool. Anyone in the community can chip in towards the next one.

Basically wanted to see if we could turn a game everyone's watching into something people can play for free and compete for real rewards.
